# Adaptive Dating Self-Discovery Quiz

An intelligent, adaptive quiz designed to help people who aren't sure what they want in dating to discover their true preferences, values, and relationship readiness.

## Features

### 🎯 Intelligent Adaptive Logic
- Optional personality framework questions (MBTI, Enneagram, Love Languages, Attachment Style)
- Questions adapt based on your answers
- Branching logic explores patterns and contradictions
- 24-34 questions that dig deep into your dating psychology and personality

### 🧠 Comprehensive Analysis
The quiz analyzes multiple dimensions:
- **Attachment Style** (anxious, avoidant, secure)
- **Relationship Goals** (casual, serious, exploring)
- **Communication Style** (direct, processing, avoiding, emotional)
- **Core Values** (authenticity, growth, independence, depth, stability)
- **Emotional Readiness**
- **Deal-breakers & Must-haves**
- **Life Priorities**

### 📊 Visual Results Dashboard
After completion, you receive:
- **Your Dater Type** - A personalized profile of how you approach dating
- **Personality Framework Insights** - How your MBTI, Enneagram, Love Language, and Attachment Style influence your dating (if provided)
- **What You're Looking For** - Deep insights into your actual desires
- **Relationship Readiness Score** - Honest assessment with strengths & growth areas
- **Core Values** - What truly matters to you in relationships
- **Communication Style** - How you handle conflict and connection
- **Dating Dimensions Chart** - Visual representation of your profile
- **Bio Recommendations** - Natural, non-cheesy suggestions you can actually use
- **Red Flags to Watch For** - Based on your patterns
- **Green Flags to Look For** - What you truly need
- **Actionable Next Steps** - Personalized guidance

### ✨ Key Benefits

- **Honest Self-Discovery**: No judgment, just truth about what you want and need
- **Pattern Recognition**: Understand why you choose who you choose
- **Practical Insights**: Usable recommendations for your dating profile
- **Natural Language**: All suggestions sound like you, not a robot
- **Readiness Assessment**: Honest look at what you're truly ready for vs. what you think you want

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Take 10-15 minutes to answer honestly
3. Review your comprehensive results dashboard
4. Use the bio recommendations on your dating profiles
5. Take the actionable steps to improve your dating life
